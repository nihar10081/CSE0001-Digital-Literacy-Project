# Social Media Do's and Don'ts for College Students

**Do's:**
1. Do use privacy settings to control who can see your personal posts and information.
2. Do showcase your academic projects, coding achievements, and campus involvement.
3. Do verify facts and sources before sharing news articles or announcements.
4. Do engage respectfully in comments and debates, remembering your digital footprint is permanent.
5. Do separate your professional profiles (LinkedIn) from your casual social media (Instagram/Snapchat).

**Don'ts:**
1. Don't post confidential university information, ID cards, or sensitive personal data.
2. Don't engage in cyberbullying, harassment, or use offensive language.
3. Don't post photos of peers or professors without their explicit consent.
4. Don't complain publicly about specific professors, college policies, or future employers.
5. Don't click on unverified links or participate in chain messages that ask for personal info.
